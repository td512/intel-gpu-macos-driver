## Hedef / Target

Tüm ölçütler aynı donanımda, Intel'in Windows sürücüsüne karşı ölçülür.

1. **Ekran:** tek monitör — modlar, VRR, uyku/uyanma, hot-plug Windows'taki gibi davranır.
2. **3B:** aynı oyun, aynı nokta, aynı ayarlar → fps ≥ Windows'un %95'i; Vulkan CTS askıda kalmadan çalışır.
3. **Video:** çözme bit-birebir ve hızı ≥ Windows; kodlama kalitesi aynı bit hızında Intel QSV'den kötü değil (QSV'ye göre BD-rate ≤ 0) ve bit hızı aşımı ≤ %5.
4. **Kararlılık:** GPU sıfırlaması masaüstünü bozmaz; uzun çalışmalarda çökme yok.

İkinci monitör şimdilik kapsam dışı. Windows referansı henüz ölçülmemiş satırlar "Windows ölçümü bekleniyor" der.

## What works on the B580

(README'ın 2026-10-31'e kadarki "What works" bölümü, olduğu gibi / the README's "What works" section up to 2026-10-31, as it was; the newest results are in the tables above and in [docs/parity/HISTORY.md](../parity/HISTORY.md).)

Measured on 2026-10-28/29 on the test machine (Arc B580 12 GB, 2560×1440 180 Hz DisplayPort monitor):

- **GPU bring-up:**
  - GuC 70.72.1 with SLPC (idle 1200 MHz, load ~2.7–2.85 GHz) and GuC RC;
  - HuC 8.2.10 authenticated;
  - all engines run jobs through GuC submission: RCS, BCS, CCS×2, VCS×2;
  - MSI interrupts;
  - idle job start ≈ 73 µs, Vulkan submit round trip ≈ 114 µs.
- **Vulkan (Mesa ANV on macOS):** device creation and work on every queue family (graphics, compute, copy, video decode and encode).
- **Metal:**
  - the B580 appears as an ordinary Metal device;
  - our Metal test suite passes 7/7;
  - FMA 3.18 TFLOPS;
  - memory bandwidth 397 GB/s with random data at 256 MB (87 % of the 456 GB/s peak).
- **Geekbench 7** (CLI, B580 selected): on macOS 26.7.1 with IntelXe (5 Nov) OpenCL 85 719 / 86 920, Metal 85 514 / 92 898; earlier on Sequoia (29 Oct, older stack) OpenCL 84 235, Metal 85 548.
- **Accelerated desktop:**
  - WindowServer composites on the B580 through IOPresentment display-pipe transactions, with real page flips. User-tested: login, windows, Safari, Mission Control, transparency.
  - 177–178 frames/s on the 180 Hz panel, measured during virtual mouse movement at the login window.
- **Display:**
  - 2560×1440 at 180 Hz over DP (HBR2 ×4);
  - 25 modes (5 sizes, scaled through the pipe scaler, × 180/144/120/100/60 Hz);
  - opt-in HiDPI modes (0.1.3): 5 "looks like" sizes at 165/180 Hz and Variable, rendered at twice the size and scaled on the GPU;
  - EDID over AUX, link retraining and full modeset (C20 PHY);
  - hardware cursor, hardware gamma;
  - Adaptive-Sync with low-framerate compensation;
  - display sleep and wake.
- **OpenGL and OpenCL:** run through Apple's GL-over-Metal (GL 4.1) and OpenCL-over-Metal. Minecraft 26.3 runs at 98–100 frames/s (p50 10.1 ms), with no glitches after the implicit-sync fixes.
- **Video (ffmpeg, Vulkan hwaccel), 1080p:**
  - H.264 decode ≈ 1030 frames/s and H.265 decode about 38× realtime, both bit-exact against software decoding;
  - AV1 decode about 46× realtime;
  - HEVC encode 240–645 frames/s, depending on the rate control.

**Added on 2026-10-29 (afternoon):**
- **CPU-visible VRAM (the 1 GB BAR window):** allocations no longer fail when the window is full: page tables have a reserve, BOs spill to system memory, a CPU map of a BO that cannot enter the window moves it to system memory, and CPU unmaps work. `debug.intelxe.vram` shows who holds the window. The Metal plugin's descriptor pools went from 77 MB of the window per drawing process to ~5 MB at first (they grow with the need), and a pool now holds the sets it was made for.
- **No silent drops:** a draw the plugin cannot record ends its command buffer with a Metal error instead of being left out.
- **VideoToolbox:** the AV1 decoder and HEVC/H.264/AV1 encoders are registered system-wide by the plugin; FFmpeg is shared (patched since 2026-10-30, see below).
- **VRAM-shared IOSurfaces** (kernel, NMXAccel, plugin, Mesa 0014): in place, off by default (`debug.intelxe.surfvram`).
- **Boot guard:** "healthy" now needs WindowServer itself (IntelXeFB v10c).

**Added on 2026-10-29 (evening) and 2026-10-30:**
- **GPU reset recovery:** the GuC's register save/restore lists are filled as Linux does it, so an engine reset no longer wipes the workarounds for every client (the cause of the "green dots" desktop corruption). A hung or banned queue is replaced on the same VM (Mesa `0050`, plugin recover1): the process keeps running, and WindowServer survives a GT reset without a logout.
- **Native Vulkan presentation:** `VK_EXT_metal_surface` swapchains on IOSurfaces with zero-copy present and Tile4 images (Mesa `0019`–`0024`). Minecraft 26.3 on its Vulkan backend runs at a median of 1329 frames/s at the spot where Windows gives 1580.
- **Browsers:** Chrome/Electron get the B580 inside their GPU sandbox; Safari's WebGL runs on the B580 with the optional system-volume copy of the plugin (`user/tools/ssv-install.sh`, plugin sc6dn).
- **Shaders:** Metal function constants become SPIR-V specialization constants (speccst6), so Core Animation's shader variants no longer cost a full translation each.
- **Video:** H.264 and HEVC VideoToolbox decoders on the B580; encode programmed as Intel's media-driver does it on Xe2 (HEVC RDOQ, H.264 B frames and BRC slice states, AV1 CDEF from the BRC); HEVC is ahead of x265 medium in VMAF BD-rate. AV1 encode got one B frame with the BRC's GOP model and two PAK passes: against SVT-AV1 on Netflix Aerial (1080p60, 3/6/12 Mbit/s) BD-rate PSNR-Y +161.8% → +135.7% and VMAF +89.8% → +68.6%; it still overshoots by 16–18% at the lowest rate, and it is still behind SVT-AV1.
- **Sleep:** IntelXeFB v10d follows IOGraphics' power protocol (WindowServer hung after the first wake with v10c) and rb6c re-requests forcewake at resume. With rb7d + v10k (plane colour control rewritten after D3cold, cursor image in a kernel display buffer) a short sleep test and two long sleeps were clean on 2026-10-30; not re-tested on the current rb7j + v10l.
- **Diagnostics:** read-only report collectors (`tools/report/`) and a staged probe mode for other Intel GPUs (XeProbe, `docs/PROBE-MODE.md`).
